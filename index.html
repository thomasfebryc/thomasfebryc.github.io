About Me

Who I Am

I'm Thomas Febry Cahyono, a final-year Software Engineering student at Universitas Bina Sarana Informatika, maintaining a 3.75 / 4.00 GPA, and currently working as a Junior Software Engineer at PT Tentakel Integrasi Indonesia.

Before transitioning into software development, I built a foundation in mechanical engineering — spanning CNC machining, CAM programming, and welding. That background instilled a systematic, precision-driven approach to problem-solving that I carry into every line of code I write.

Today I focus on crafting web applications and Internet of Things (IoT) systems, connecting devices like ESP32, Arduino, and Raspberry Pi to larger platforms. I enjoy debugging more than most people — tracing the root cause of a problem rather than patching the visible symptom — and I lead with a "wake up, speak up, stand up" culture, where issues get raised early instead of bottled up until the deadline is looming.
